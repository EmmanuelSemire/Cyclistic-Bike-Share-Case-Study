# Cyclistic Bike-Share Analysis: Executive Case Study

## 📌 Executive Summary
Cyclistic’s finance team identified that annual members are significantly more profitable than casual riders. To drive sustainable growth, the marketing team aims to convert casual riders into annual members.

This analysis evaluates historical trip data from **Q1 (791,956 total rides)** to identify core behavioral differences between annual members (**91.0% share**) and casual riders (**9.0% share**) and formulate targeted conversion strategies.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Data Analytics & Modeling:** Power BI, DAX, Microsoft Excel
* **Documentation & Presentation:** Microsoft Word, Microsoft PowerPoint

---

## 🔑 Key Analytical Insights

1. **Usage Patterns & Day-of-Week Trends:**
   * **Annual Members:** Act as weekday commuters, peaking Monday through Friday (~110k–135k rides/day) and dropping on weekends (~60k rides).
   * **Casual Riders:** Act as weekend recreational riders, peaking on Saturday and Sunday (~13k–18k rides/day) with low weekday engagement.

2. **Trip Duration Disparity:**
   * **Casual Riders:** Average trip duration is **84.8 minutes**, driven by leisure and extended recreational journeys.
   * **Annual Members:** Average trip duration is **13.2 minutes**, reflecting short, utility-focused commutes.
   * **Key Takeaway:** Casual riders ride approximately **6x longer per trip** than annual members.

---

## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations (Act Phase)

1. **Targeted Weekend & Leisure Marketing Campaigns**
   * Launch targeted physical and digital ads near popular parks, recreational hubs, and waterfront routes during peak weekend hours (Friday–Sunday).

2. **In-App Post-Ride "Savings Calculator"**
   * Deliver automated push notifications following long casual rides (>30 mins) illustrating exact savings achieved under an annual membership plan.

3. **Flexible Pass-to-Member Conversion Credits**
   * Allow casual users to credit single-pass or weekend pass purchases toward an annual membership upgrade within 30 days.
